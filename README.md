# Retail Sales Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project presents a **Retail Sales Performance Dashboard** built to analyze business performance across multiple dimensions including **sales, profit, regions, categories, and customer segments**.

The dashboard provides insights that help businesses understand revenue trends, profitability, and operational performance over time.

# Key Insights

- The business generated **$33.9M in total sales** with **$7.8M total profit**, achieving a **23% profit margin**.
- The **East and West regions contribute the largest share of total revenue**.
- The **Consumer segment drives the majority of total sales**.
- **Electronics and Furniture categories dominate revenue generation**.
- Seasonal patterns show stronger performance during certain months, highlighting demand fluctuations.
